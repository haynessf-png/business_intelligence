# Airbnb Market Analysis

1. Across the three cities, 198 hosts have at least 10 listings, showing that some hosts operate large portfolios.
2. In Chicago, hosts with at least 10 listings hold 31.9% of listings, meaning nearly one-third of its listings belong to large hosts.
3. Columbus's top 10% of listings earn 36.1% of estimated revenue, showing that earnings are concentrated among a small share of listings.
4. Twin Cities listings received 7,399 reviews in August 2025, making it the busiest review month from July 2025 through June 2026.
5. Chicago has 267 listings with no availability rows, the most of the three cities, so analyses requiring availability data leave out the most listings there.
